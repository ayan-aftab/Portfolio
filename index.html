// fun fact

Born to drive history.

⚡ Favourite Hobby: I love learning the history of cars, the machines and the engineering behind them, not the people.
🏎️
1769

Cugnot’s steam wagon

The first self-propelled road vehicle was a steam-powered French artillery tractor, moving at roughly walking speed.

1886

Benz Patent-Motorwagen

A three-wheeler with a single-cylinder petrol engine, patented in 1886 and widely seen as the first true automobile.

1908

Ford Model T

Built to be affordable for ordinary families, it put the world on wheels, with over 15 million made by 1927.

1913

The moving assembly line

Ford’s line cut the time to build a Model T from more than 12 hours to about 90 minutes.

1938

Volkswagen Beetle

Its design was set in the late 1930s and it kept going until 2003, with more than 21 million built.

1959

Volvo’s three-point seatbelt

Volvo gave the patent away to every carmaker, and it estimates the belt has saved over a million lives.

1972

BMW M is born

BMW Motorsport GmbH was founded to go racing. Its first road car, the mid-engined M1, arrived in 1978.

1997

Toyota Prius

The first mass-produced hybrid car went on sale in Japan, starting the shift toward electrified driving.
